# Corruption predictor

On the Corruption page, **Optimise for** defaults to **Auto**, which follows the highest resource the player can earn within their unlocked corruption range. The choices are cubes, tesseracts, hypercubes, platonic cubes and hepteracts. An explicit choice is remembered across reloads and shared with the corruption quickbar. If that resource locks again, the choice stays selected and the player can switch to an available resource or return to Auto.

**Find optimal level** compares the selected resource's relative ascension yield. Three short C1–C10 sweeps of 0.5 real seconds per challenge build up completion bonuses, then a 20-second pass gives slower challenges more time. There is a 0.1-second gap between visits. A challenge that caps early spends the rest of its visit outside the challenge.

**Use level** changes the next ascension's corruption through the game's controls. Calculating never changes the live player or runs an ascension. Ordinary progression within the same singularity keeps the result usable. A save import, singularity reset, Exalt transition or objective change requires a fresh estimate. Applying requires a loaded game, corruption unlocked, Auto-climb off and Autosing stopped. The game still checks the allowed level.

After C11, open the [corruption quickbar](header-corruption.md) with the arrow beside the corruption icon in the header. Choose a resource by its icon, then press **Set best** to calculate and apply in one action. The same application checks apply.

## Exalts

All ten Exalts support the next-ascension estimate after clearing C11 again. Active C11–C15 challenges overwrite corruption, so leave the ascension challenge before estimating.

- One Challenge Caps limits new C1–C10 completions to one. The simulation and payout ceiling preserve starting grants, including five C8 completions with the Singularity 15 perk.
- No Golden Quark Upgrades, Disappearance of a Salesman, No Octeract Effects, No Ambrosia Upgrades and Sadistic Prequel use the effective upgrades for the active Exalt. Instant Challenge I and II are Utility upgrades in Salesman.
- Limited Ascensions uses the next ascension count for its speed penalty. The Grand Singularity Speedrun applies its elapsed-time penalty to offering income and its Reborn ELO bonus before Antiquities. The sweep takes the same wall time at each candidate level, so the common reward penalty cancels in the relative payout.
- Taxman’s Last Stand applies its tax and offering penalties, its tier 2 cap on offering grants, its tier 5 tribute penalty, and its tier 8 Challenge 13 rules for taxes, ant speed and completion cadence. Its Obtainium-based offering penalty uses the saved balance as a fixed estimate.
- Bar Charging estimates reset and ascension time from sustained blue and red bar rewards, including Purple Reactor fills. Individual bar fills and their timing can differ from that average.

## Startup estimate

The model is intended to help choose levels early in a singularity. It requires corruption unlocked and Instant Challenge II. Each candidate gets independent currencies, purchased producers, generated producer chains and ordinary upgrades. The next ascension seeds lower-layer balances from singularity perks and free building milestones. Visits reset the relevant lower layers, award the corresponding reset currency, repurchase available upgrades and buildings, and advance crystal, mythos and particle production. Conversion upgrades let particle producers generate mythos producers and mythos producers generate crystal producers. Entering reincarnation challenges clears C1–C5. The auto-completion researches can restore previously reached completions during the visit.

Coin production uses the candidate's construction, viscosity and recession effects, active-challenge penalties, and the tax formula. The tax ceiling is an upper bound on actual production, never an assumed output. C1–C5 check coins earned in that transcension, C6–C8 check produced transcend shards, and C9–C10 check current coins. C6, C8 and C10 rewards feed back into production. C6–C8 are not granted their caps. Ordinary upgrades and producer purchases spend the simulated currencies. C9 clears crystal upgrades on entry, and unlocked milestone autobuyers can purchase them again during the visit. Cost curves include quadratic producer scaling and accelerator/multiplier/boost cubic and late softcap scaling.

Active visits use 25 ms steps during startup and 250 ms steps after the first second. The first scan holds runes and ants at the input snapshot. A detailed pass reruns the best three levels, their neighbors and any eligible current run. It starts reset-sensitive runes and ants from ascension grants, advances ant production, autobuys and sacrifices, and spends offerings from resets and ant sacrifices on runes, blessings and spirits according to the player's automation settings. Rune EXP uses the ascension clock, including One Mind. Reset and ant-sacrifice clocks use Half Mind when unlocked. The Superior Intellect blessing uses simulated blessing power and the saved Obtainium balance as a fixed estimate. Research, talismans, campaigns, loadouts, achievements and higher progression stay fixed. The production model approximates offering multipliers, purchase timing and the spending split among coin producers.

The patched game exposes its pending ascension payout. If auto-opening is enabled, the predictor adds the expected tributes from the pending payout and eligible saved balances to the calculation snapshot, including conversions from higher cube types. This omits spending on upgrades and tesseract buildings before auto-opening. Older loaders and the bookmarklet use the current tribute totals.

## Rewards and inputs

The search prefers the lower level on ties and requires a C10 completion and a resource unlocked at that level. If the detailed pass finds no eligible payout, the first scan supplies the result. If the current run has capped C10, its actual completion counts can also compete at the corruption level credited for that ascension, independently of the simulated C10 cutoff. The result labels those counts as the current run's. The ranking uses cube bank × the chosen resource's corruption rate × its corruption-dependent Platonic bonus, including C9's OMEGA bonus, Tau and Cube Upgrade 4x8 for tesseracts. Common fixed multipliers cancel. Payout rounding and saturation are outside the estimate.

Challenge caps include all three Golden Quark challenge extensions, whose full levels add 20 reincarnation completions. The live snapshot preserves Decimal mantissas and exponents beyond the JSON serializer's range. The game restores an exported `"Infinity"` as a sentinel, which the predictor rejects until the game has a usable live shard value.

Each search sends its save and GDS context to two background workers (one on single-threaded devices). The fast pass scans ranges of up to 16 levels in full. On larger ranges it samples roughly eight intervals. When the sampled levels show a consistent C10 cutoff, the workers narrow the transition and check through five levels above the first zero before dropping the upper tail. A sampled C10 recovery disables the cutoff. If fewer than three eligible results remain, the search expands back to the full range. The all-capped payout bounds each level's maximum score. Once a level cannot place in the top three even with every challenge capped, its simulation can be skipped.

The workers take small batches from the search, then rerun candidates from one shared shortlist. The current-level comparison also runs in a worker. The main thread ranks results and applies the chosen level through the game's controls. The worker build shares Heater's embedded-source plugin. Browsers that block workers show an error.

## In-game checks

1. With the development build loaded, open Ascension → Corruptions. On a save with no explicit preference, expect **Auto · Tesseracts** after clearing C11 with no higher resource earned. With hepteracts unlocked and corruption 20 available, expect **Auto · Hepteracts**. Choose Cubes and calculate. The comparison should name Cubes, and the choice should survive a reload.
2. Note current and next levels, calculate, then leave the game progressing for several seconds. Calculation must leave both levels unchanged. With Auto-climb and Autosing off, Use level N must still set next to N without asking to recalculate because rune EXP or upgrades changed. Current must stay unchanged.
3. With the 150-cap GQ upgrades bought, the modeled C6–C10 caps must be 150, not 130. Predicted completions may be below the cap if production is insufficient.
4. In the headless game harness, take early and later checkpoints after resetting a save. Replay nearby levels from each checkpoint and record C1–C10 after each sweep and the final payout. A run with zero C10 completions earns no ascension payout. The native rewards preview alone can report a positive value in that state.
5. Enable Auto-climb or start Autosing and try applying. Expect matching guidance and no level changes. While inside C11–C15, calculating asks you to leave the ascension challenge.
6. Click the arrow beside the corruption icon in the header after C11. The arrow points up and the predictor appears below the level controls. Selecting an icon updates **Optimise for** on the Corruption page. With Auto-climb and Autosing off, **Set best** sets exactly the level reported by the page for the same resource and snapshot. The current level and ascension count stay the same. Escape closes the quickbar and the arrow points down again.
7. Start **Find optimal level** and switch game tabs while it runs. The tabs should respond immediately. Load a save while **Set best** is running, or after a page estimate but before pressing **Use level**. The old estimate must never set the next corruption level.
8. In an Exalt, clear C11 and estimate Cubes. The Corruption page and **Set best** must agree. Applying changes only the next level. In One Challenge Caps expect at most one newly earned completion per C1–C10, with the post-ascension C8 grant still reaching five if owned. Leave the Exalt after calculating and try **Use level**. The previous result must be rejected.
