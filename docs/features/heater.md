# Ambrosia Heater

## Heater UI and logging rules

- `hs-modules/hs-heater/`: Ambrosia Heater loadout optimizer. It runs in a web worker built separately (`hs-heater-optimizer-worker.ts`).
  - **A heat is logged in the Log tab** (`HSHeaterOptimizerRunner`): one summary line with a time per worker group, or "result reused" for an input already heated. With debug on, one more line per group with the optimizer's stage times (`Heat stages, cubes+sr: …`, stages under 0.05 s left out; a parent stage comes after its sub-stages): the worker calls `runExperiment` and sends `diagnostics.stages` back (2026-10-29, Shewchou's choice; no stage on the heat button, it is full). The optimizer can't import `HSLogger` (no DOM in a worker): its stand-in keeps `warn` and `error` lines (`takeHeaterOptimizerLogs()`), the worker sends them back with its answer, the runner writes them. A failed heat logs its input as JSON (runner) and the stack (the heat button's catch). The optimizer's crash dump stays console only. A line given to `HSLogger` is rendered as HTML and also printed to the console: escape it with `escapeLogText` (`&` and `<` only), not `escapeHtml`.
  - **A heat can be cancelled** (✖ right of "Heating…", shown only while the optimizer runs in a worker): `HSHeaterOptimizerRunner.cancel()` terminates the running worker, `createResult` rejects with `HSHeatCancelledError`, nothing is cached and the groups already heated are dropped (Shewchou's choice). One "Heat cancelled" log line, no notification.
  - **Two workers** (2026-10-29, Shewchou's choices): "Faster heat (2 workers)" checkbox right under the heat button (hidden setting `heaterTwoWorkers`, on by default; hidden without a worker), its warning in the title. `BRANCH_GROUPS` gives each group a lane: lane 0 Hyperflux then Cubes+SR (the two big ones, ~0.8 GB each, never at once), lane 1 Oct+AmbOct, Luck+Purple, Quarks/Obt/Off; each lane runs its groups one at a time. S260 node: 96 → ~57 s, peak ~1.3 GB summed instead of 0.9; in Chrome's Task Manager Shewchou saw at most 1.1 GB on their own save at 144M Ambrosia, 1.05 GB at 110M (2026-10-29). Falls back to one worker when `navigator.deviceMemory` < 8 or `hardwareConcurrency` < 4 (unknown = enough), said in the summary line. A failing group stops the other lane; a cancel stops both. Results are merged by group index, so the order doesn't depend on which lane ends first. Max Amb + Oct moved from the Luck group to the Oct group the same day (shares Oct's tables; same row, ~2.6 s saved when affordable).
  - **The heat button shows the elapsed time** in whole seconds, and the number of worker groups started when there are several (`🔥 Heating 2/3... ⏳ 12s 🌡️`, from `createResult`'s `onProgress`, called before each group). The last icon follows the time: 🌡️ under 60 s, 😵 from 60 s, 🤒 from 90 s, 🥵 from 120 s. It only ticks with a worker; without one the page is frozen and the label stays as it is.
  - A result row's Effect is text, formatted in the worker by `formatNumber` (`hs-heater-utils.ts`) with the browser's number format; the results renderer prints it as is.
  - A type the player can't afford is still in the results, as an "Unaffordable" row with no loadout. In "Your loadout order", a red border and an unticked checkbox mean the type is missing from the results (its branch wasn't heated); an orange border and an unticked checkbox mean Unaffordable. "Import All" skips an unticked slot silently and leaves it as it is; an Unaffordable type the player ticked again is skipped with a warning (notification and log).
  - **An Unaffordable row names the short resource** (2026-10-27, Shewchou chose the columns over a tooltip): its Blueberries and/or Cost cell shows what the tree needs, in the ✘ red, with "You have N" as title; a resource that isn't short stays "N / A". The worker sets it (`unaffordableRow(needed, atLeast)`, the row's optional 8th element `shortfall`). Exact for Max Amb + Oct (the full All Ambrosia base, a valid row on its own) and `generateOutput`'s checks; a minimum, shown "≥", for H rows (Hyperflux at that level alone) and SR1 (SR1 + its Hyperflux 4). Neither short (no tree fits for another reason): both "N / A". A type picked in "Your loadout order" and Unaffordable has its whole row in the orange of that slot's border (`#c96a00`, red before).
  - **A ✘ row says what limits it** (2026-10-27, icon version chosen; the richer "next step" title was declined): the game's Ambrosia and/or Blueberry icon after the ✘, titles "More Ambrosia would raise it" / "More Blueberries would raise it"; both = either would. `Loadout.limitingResources` tries +1 level of each upgrade (prerequisites included, never above `maxLoadout`'s levels) on a copy: a stat-raising try that needs more Blueberries than are left → Blueberries; one whose Blueberries fit but not its Ambrosia → Ambrosia. No measurable time (node bench, S260 save). The renderer shows them only on ✘: the bar-income rows whose ✔ is set afterwards can carry them. An Unaffordable row's ✘ takes the same icons from its `shortfall` (renderer only), titled "Not enough Ambrosia" / "Not enough Blueberries" (2026-10-27, Shewchou's choice: there, both = both needed); no shortfall → bare ✘.
  - **Max SR1 / Max SR2 rows** (2026-10-28, Shewchou's choices): the Effect is the tree's cube multiplier, like the H rows (Hyperflux at P4x4 50, Tau), so players can see what maxing SR costs in cubes (`generateOutput`'s `effectStat`). "Maxed?" stays "SR at its max level". The Types cell shows the SR level ("Max SR1 (1/2)", `labelLevel` in the type config, read from the row's tree by the renderer; the select labels and semantic ids don't change). The Effect's title gives the penalty SR lifts (Obtainium/Offerings and Cubes factors, `withPenaltyReliefTitle`, row element 8). SR1 is heated as outside an Exalt and SR2 as inside one, whatever the heat's Exalt: their descriptions say so. No SR level within budget: Unaffordable. The H rows already take SR when it raises cubes, so Max SR1 is often the very H7 tree.
  - **Max SR rows keep an inexact search on purpose** (2026-10-29, Shewchou's choice): `findCubeLuckOpt`'s sampled upper bound assumed Luck-Cube rows score more as they cost more, false on that Ambrosia × Blueberries frontier; it dropped the best tree (a player's save: Cubes ×0.89 with 11 Blueberries and 20M Ambrosia left, H0-H3 too). Cubes, Oct and the Hyperflux rows no longer use it (exact). SR1/SR2 pass `sampledUpperBound`: their SR level is set before the search and their cubes are a bonus, while without the bound their Blueberry repairs took the SR stage from 8 to 29 s (S260 save).
  - **"Maxed?" compares with a tree that can exist**: an H row's maxed tree drops upgrades whose prerequisites its Hyperflux level doesn't meet (`dropUnmetPrerequisites`): SR1 needs Hyperflux 4 and raises Cube pre-AoAG, so H0-H3 were always ✘ before 2026-10-27.
  - The four Purple types are the last rows of "Common Loadouts" (no Purple section). When one of them is in the results, that section's Effect and Routing are one cell spanning both effect columns, split by CSS (`.hs-heater-split-cell`), so the routing's width doesn't set the p4x4 section's columns; the other rows leave the routing empty. With no Purple row, the section has no Routing column.
  - The "Purple Ambrosia" branch checkbox (inputs modal) is only there when the save has an Exalt 9 completion (`exalt9Unlocked` in the heater data, the game's own unlock condition); otherwise the branch is off.
  - **Three grey glyphs at the right end of the "Active Branches:" line** (2026-10-28, Shewchou's choices: two glyphs rather than one toggle, the slots one added first): ☰ ticks exactly the branches of the slots that have a type and unticks the rest, ☑ ticks all, ☐ unticks all. They act on the checkboxes shown only (a locked Purple is left alone), and save like a click.
  - **The branch checkboxes are remembered** (2026-10-28, Shewchou's choices): the hidden setting `heaterUncheckedBranches` holds the branches the player unticked; a branch never unticked starts ticked (first open, a new branch, Purple once unlocked). Setting a slot's type ticks that type's branch (Best H = Hyperflux), and that is saved too. Only that slot's branch, and nothing at open: slots change only in the modal, so ticking the other slots' branches could only undo the player's untick (the first version did both; Shewchou saw Quarks and Oct re-ticked at open). Nothing is ever unticked for the player; unticking a branch a slot needs is allowed (the slot's red border after the heat says it). The red background + 🔥 on a branch a slot needed (same test as the slot's red border) was removed the same day.
  - **The Exalt badge** (left of the results topbar, opposite `?`) shows when the heat's `input.exalt` has an entry in `HEATER_EXALT_RULES` (`hs-heater-result-config.ts`), today only Exalt 10; its tooltip says how the results read in that Exalt. Shewchou chose this over piling Exalt rules into the type descriptions (2026-10-20). Other Exalts get an entry only after checking what the optimizer does in them against the game.
  - **Exalts are shown by name, never by number** (`HEATER_EXALT_OPTIONS` / `getHeaterExaltName` in `hs-heater-input-config.ts`; the select, the two "x1 Done" inputs, the warning, the Exalt badge): the game's numbers disagree with each other (code order, unlock order, and its own text calls No Ambrosia Upgrades "EXALT 5"). The values stay GDS's `getActiveExalt()` numbers (by unlock singularity), which the optimizer's rules use (Shewchou's choice, 2026-10-21).
  - **The Exalt select drives every Exalt rule of the optimizer**, No Quark Upgrades included: `shopUpgradesDisabled` is `exalt === 4` in the worker, no longer the export's flag (GDS still exports it, unread). The base fields (luck, bar speed, quark, cubes…) still come from the exported save as it was, so a line under the select ("⚠️ Base values come from a save in <Exalt name>" / "outside any Exalt") shows while the picked Exalt differs from the export's `exalt` (`HSHeaterUIInput.refreshExaltWarning`; Shewchou chose it in the modal over a notification, 2026-10-21). Removing the shop part from the bases (export shop-free values) was left out.

## Inputs modal vs hidden inputs

**Field order** (2026-10-29, Shewchou's choice): players rarely read the inputs, so the few they may change on purpose come first under "Heat for" (Exalt?, Current Singularity, Active Bells, Event Blue Bar Bonus, Post-AoAG); no more fields there. Then Blue Ambrosia, Red Ambrosia (bar fields with their luck/speed), Red Ambrosia Upgrades, Resources & Singularity, Runes & Talismans, Unlocks (moved down from second, Shewchou's choice), Shop last. A grey heading row comes from `heading` on a group's first field in `inputDefinitions`; nothing reads the list by position.

Since 2026-10-29 every modal field reaches the optimizer. The optimizer had moved to hidden export data (`shopUpgradeRawLevels`, `shopBonusLevelsNoAmbrosia`, `panthemaLevel`) and left 15 fields doing nothing (Jack, Free Shop Q. Levels, Shop Red Luck 1–4, Shop Ambrosia Gen 1–4, Shop QHept 1–∞); Free Infinity Upgrades only reached the Red Ambrosia table. Now `fillStatsAndOptionsFromInput` overlays the modal's shop levels onto `shopUpgradeRawLevels` and its Free Quark/Infinity levels onto the Quark/Infinity groups (GDS exports equal values on both sides, checked on 8 backups), and "Jack of All Trades Level" (`panthemaLevel`) replaced the Jack checkbox. GDS still exports `jack` (unread). The inputs left out of the modal on purpose, with the reasons Shewchou agreed to, are listed in the `HeaterInputBase` comment (`hs-heater-input-config.ts`).

## Platonic Tau

The game raises every cube multiplier (Wow! Cubes to Octeracts) to Tau's power (1.01 once bought, kept in every Exalt: QoL upgrade) since 2026-10-26 (`applyTauPower`, Calculate.ts). The heater's `cube`/`oct` stats stay untaued: the merge shortcuts multiply them by factors, and r^1.01 keeps every cube or oct ranking (H thresholds included). Tau (`tauPower` in the heater data, default 1) is applied only where it changes something: the `ambOct` score (allAmb × oct^tau) and the Effect shown on cube/oct rows (`generateOutput`). The Red Ambrosia table's Octeract effects don't apply it (same order, values ~1% low).

## Singularity penalty (SR relief)

`Upgrade.singDebuff` mirrors the game's `calculateSingularityDebuff` and is only used as a ratio, singDebuff(s) / singDebuff(s − SR level). `mOff` is the Offering/Obtainium penalty times Global Speed's penalty once (until 2026-10-28 it counted Global Speed twice, from the rus sync, which overstated SR's Obtainium/Offerings relief by ×1.13 to ×5). `cube` is the Cubes penalty times Ascension Speed's. Left out on purpose, since they cancel in the ratio (checked 2026-10-28, under 1e-5): the HorseShoe shop multiplier, and No Octeract Effects' (completions + 1)³ on effective singularities (not in the export).

Obtainium's diminishing returns (`illiteracy`, `calculateObtainium`) are left out too: they are ×1 outside Challenge 15. Inside it they raise the product of Obtainium multipliers to ^0.2–0.4 (Global Speed and Red Ambrosia excluded), which shrinks SR's penalty relief and Ambrosia Obtainium alike. The heater models no ascension challenge (checked 2026-10-28).

## Exalt 10 (Bar Charging)

The bars give time instead of Ambrosia (blue fill: reward × 0.01 s of prestige/transcension/reincarnation/potion time; red fill: reward × 0.05 s of ascension time; `Helper.ts`), the bar lengths are fixed (Brick of Lead doesn't lengthen them), and each Purple extraction gives both bar rewards once per 100 Purple Honey reward Luck instead of Honey.
- `calculateAmbrosiaBarIncome` keeps `blue/redAmbrosiaPerSecond` at 0 there (the header's hourly rates read them) and gives the bar rewards in Ambrosia units in `blue/redRewardPerSecond`. The heater's Blue, Red and All scores read the reward fields: in Exalt 10 they mean global time, ascension time and their product (constant factors don't change the pick). Before 2026-10-20 every loadout scored 0 there and the heater handed out the cheapest tree.
- With One Mind, ascension speed is a multiplier on cubes and ascension count (`Statistics.ts`, `OneMind` lines). Brick of Lead lowers it (×0.75 at max) and SR2 raises it (singularity penalty). The bar-time scores don't count it; how players weigh it against time in Exalt 10 was asked to two players (rus, Aceo) on 2026-10-20.
- Checked on a player's Exalt 10 save (slot 4 = their hand-made tree): the heater picks that tree minus SR2, with Cube-Luck 1 and Quark-Luck 1 at 25, ×5.84 against ×5.26.

## Best H (current P4x4)

- **A slot choice, not a result row**: "Best H (P4x4 now)" (`hyperflux:best`, `HEATER_BEST_H_SEMANTIC_ID`) sits after H7 in the "Your loadout order" dropdowns. `HSHeaterResultStore.resolveSemanticId` turns it into the H row that wins at the live P4x4; every reader of a slot's choice goes through it (availability borders, unaffordable check, Import All, results highlight). Sync Icons and Sync Settings keep the raw id (Hyperflux icon; Autosing Early Cube preference after H7, before Oct).
- **The rule** (`pickBestHyperfluxRowIndex`): the highest H row whose "p4x4 eq" is a number ≤ P4x4. The optimizer's thresholds keep only rows that win somewhere, with strictly rising p4x4 eq; the others show "Never", unaffordable ones "N / A". No row qualifies (all unaffordable): H0, shown as unaffordable. No H rows, or P4x4 unknown: unresolved, red border, and Import All skips it with a warning.
- **P4x4 is read live**, at Import All (once for every slot) and at each draw of the results table: `exposedPlayer.platonicUpgrades[19]`, else the last GDS save. Not the heat-time value: P4x4 (max 50) resets at every singularity and climbs during it, and long sings (new highest, Exalts) make a mid-sing pick useful (Shewchou's choice, 2026-10-20). The pick is made once: nothing re-picks when P4x4 crosses a threshold later (backlog, not wanted for now).
- **"★ now"** in the p4x4 eq cell marks that row in the results; its title gives the P4x4 used. The table is redrawn at each heat and slot change, not when P4x4 moves. Import All notifies and logs "Best H at P4x4 N: L3: H5".
- **To test it**, change P4x4 through the Debug tab's live edits, then change a slot dropdown to redraw the results.

## Happy Hour bells (Active Bells field)

Since 2026-10-29 (option C, Shewchou's choice): the heat is for the **Active Bells** field's bell count (default: the live count, so a default heat is unchanged), e.g. 0 to heat for outside Happy Hour.
- Bells change three heater inputs: the additive luck mult (+0.1 + 0.01·(n−1)), blue bar speed (×(1 + g + same)/(1 + g), g = the global event's BlueberryTime part, one factor in `Statistics.ts` 'Event'), and Base Red Luck through its share of Ambrosia luck (floor((luck − 100) / conversion), `calculateRedAmbrosiaLuck`). Quark, Cubes, Offering and Obtainium get their own `1 + buff` factor, the same on every loadout: no effect on picks. Red bar speed: none (no event line, no blue speed term).
- GDS exports the three without bells (`totalAdditiveLuckMultiplierNoAmbNoBells`, `finalAmbrosiaBarPointsSNoAmbNoBells`, `totalRedLuckNoAmbNoBells`: the terms GDS added, taken out) and g (`globalEventBlueBarBonus`); the old keys stay. The three fields are labelled "(no bells)"; g is the "Event Blue Bar Bonus" field.
- `applyHeaterBells` (`hs-heater-utils.ts`) adds the field's bells back; every reader of the three fields goes through it (worker's `fillStatsAndOptionsFromInput`, its `totalRedLuck` for the Red Ambrosia table and the dev diagnostics, the table's two speed reads). The table's `mLuck` comes from the worker, bells included.
- Exports are always live (`dumpDataForHeater`), so there is no fallback for an old export without the new keys.
- Rejected: the modal rewriting the three fields when Active Bells changes (A), and the optimizer correcting them from the export's hidden bell count (B). Base Red Luck was found to depend on bells only when Shewchou asked whether other fields needed "(no bells)": check every field's formula for a bell term when a new one is added.

## Speed: what was done and what was rejected

Heat time grows with the Ambrosia budget (Obt/Off: 3 s at 1M, ~150 s at 83.7M on a S230 save): the searches' early exits never fire when everything is affordable. Measure with `scripts/harness/node/heater-bench.ts` (node times match the browser's); a change must give the same rows on several backups and budgets, inside Exalts too (the bench takes `EXALT`).
- **Done:** `findResourceVoucherOpt` bounds each voucher count × Obtainium I / Offering I group and searches from the highest bound down (09b62ff5, from XxMolkxX's v2.14.4c code: quarks + Obt/Off 148 → 24 s in game). Voucher merges sort only the new batch and merge it into the sorted frontier, trimming only when the batch is at least the frontier's size (b3d45209: the time was the trim, 564 re-sorts of a ~300k frontier; cube merge ~32 → ~9 s). The sampled upper bound left Cubes, Oct and Hyperflux (f5a61d46, exactness; the cube search got faster). The Obt + Off row's exact speed-ups (above).
- **Measured and rejected:**
  - Hyperflux in the Cubes+SR worker: ~10 s saved, peak memory 864 MB → 1,314 MB. The groups are split on purpose for memory; merging groups to avoid rebuilding shared tables (~15–18 s of ~250 s) stays rejected.
  - Merging Cubes/SR1/SR2 into one `findCubeLuckOptsForBudgets` pass: not exact (Purple Leo luck moves with SR's Blueberries; strict mode skips repaired pairs). Its exact part, scoring SR copies from the Cube search, gave the same rows in 26 configurations for SR 9.4 → 5.8 s, at ~45–55 subtle lines: shelved by Shewchou.
  - "Everything affordable → max tree": on a S230 save only SR1/SR2 qualify (≤ 10 s).
  - Precomputed maxed trees per type: the cheapest tree reaching the all-max value costs 154–214M Ambrosia and 34–42 Blueberries for Quarks/Oct/Cubes; late saves have ~90M and 31 Blueberries. Luck/Red/All/Purple already have the shortcut (`findAffordableFullLevelBarIncomeOpts`, full at 64–80M); only Obt/Off would qualify (full at 83.7M / 26 Blueberries outside Exalts, sing- and Exalt-dependent). A generalised version (best tree with unlimited Ambrosia, computed at heat time since the module set depends on the save) is designed and parked: small gain today, future-proofing.
  - Same-item prefilter in voucher merges (0 rows dropped), a growing batch in `createBatchedTable` / `mergeIndependentTables` (no gain beyond noise), starting Obt + Off from the Obt/Off results (no gain).

## Known limits

- **Purple Leo luck on the Hyperflux rows H1–H7.** In `findCubeLuckOptsForBudgets` with `blueberryReserves`, the Blueberries reserved for Hyperflux (3, or 2 with its purple enchantment) still count as unassigned when a candidate is scored. Leo gives unassigned × level luck, only with 5 or more unassigned (max level 25), so a candidate can be credited up to 75 luck too much, or the whole Leo bonus when it really ends below 5 free. The numbers shown are exact (each winner is re-scored at its Hyperflux level); only the choice between near-equal trees can be off, for trees leaving 5 or more Blueberries free before Hyperflux. Fix idea: when `stats.purpleLeoLevel > 0`, correct the score in the same pass (`createCubeLuckEvaluator.evaluate`: the reserve only changes `purpleLeoDelta`, two cached scores per pair; ~30–40 lines in the hot function, no cost without Leo). Alternative: search H1+ on tables whose rows carry Hyperflux 1, only with Leo (exact, 6 passes instead of 3, about +40% on the Hyperflux stage). Test with a made-up Leo level or a Leo owner's export, against the separate-table version.
- **Unproven assumptions after f5a61d46:** the `j` pointer in `findCubeLuckOptSingleFixed` / `findCubeLuckOptsForBudgets` assumes the best Luck-Cube row never moves back to a cheaper one from one cube row to the next, on an Ambrosia × Blueberries frontier. `findQuarkLuckOptSingleFixed` and `findLuckScaledResourceOpt` (Obt/Off) keep the sampled upper bound; whether their right tables are 2D frontiers wasn't checked. To check: capture the two tables before the search (`scripts/harness/node/heater-bench.ts`), fix one side and scan the other, after estimating the pair count. Max SR1/SR2 keep the inexact bound on purpose (above).
